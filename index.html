Screen Recorder: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Screen Recorder ("the app") works entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

What the app accesses

  • Your screen. Only after you allow capture in Android's system dialog. Android asks every time you start a recording.
  • Microphone. Only when you choose "Microphone" as the audio source and grant the microphone permission.
  • Device audio (Android 10 and later). Only when you choose it. Some apps block audio capture.
  • Notifications. Used to show the ongoing-recording notification with its Pause, Resume and Stop controls.

What the app stores

  • Recordings are saved as MP4 files in Movies/ScreenRecorder on your device.
  • Your recording settings (resolution, frame rate, quality, audio source and countdown) are stored on your device.

What the app does not do

  • The app has no internet permission. Nothing is uploaded, collected, shared or sold.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Recordings leave your device only if you share them yourself, using Android's share sheet.

Your control

  • Stop a recording at any time from the notification, the status bar chip or the app.
  • Delete recordings in the app's Recordings tab or in any file manager.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Recordings already saved in Movies/ScreenRecorder stay until you delete them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
